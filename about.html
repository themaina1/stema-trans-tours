About Stema Trans

Private tours, transfers and guided experiences in Nairobi and beyond.

Who We Are

Stema Trans Tours is a Nairobi-based private transportation and tour service providing city tours, airport transfers, sightseeing experiences and customized travel around Nairobi and other destinations in Kenya.

We combine comfortable private transportation with local knowledge to help visitors and residents explore Nairobi in a convenient and relaxed way.

Whether you are arriving in Kenya for the first time, visiting Nairobi for business, spending a few days in the city or looking for a private sightseeing experience, we aim to make your journey simple, comfortable and enjoyable.

What We Do

Flexible transportation and experiences designed around your plans.

Nairobi City Tours

Discover Nairobi's landmarks, history, culture, attractions and local character with a private vehicle and driver/guide.

Airport Transfers

Reliable private transfers between JKIA, Wilson Airport, hotels, residences, offices and other destinations.

Wildlife Experiences

Experience Nairobi National Park and other wildlife attractions conveniently from the city.

Cultural & Historical Tours

Visit museums, cultural attractions and historic sites while learning more about Nairobi and Kenya.

Private Excursions

Longer journeys and customized trips can be arranged according to your destination and itinerary.

Corporate Transportation

Professional transportation for meetings, conferences, business travellers and corporate events.
