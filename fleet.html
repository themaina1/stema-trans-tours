Important Pricing Information

Vehicle hire rates are charged per vehicle, not per passenger.

The SUV, Tour Van and Tour Land Cruiser rates shown above are for the stated half-day or full-day vehicle hire for excursions and national park trips.

National park entrance fees, attraction entrance fees, activities, accommodation, meals and other applicable charges are separate unless specifically stated as included in a particular booking.

For city tours, airport transfers, hotel transfers and other services, the applicable service rate may be different from the excursion and park vehicle-hire rates shown here.

Please confirm the service, destination, duration and vehicle category when making a booking so that the correct quotation can be provided.
