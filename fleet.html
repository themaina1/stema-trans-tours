How Our Vehicle Pricing Works

Vehicle rates are charged per vehicle, not per passenger.

The listed rate covers the vehicle for the selected service within the applicable passenger capacity.

For attraction and national park visits, entrance, park and activity fees are charged separately unless the booking specifically states that they are included.

For example, a private Nairobi City Tour using a Tour Van is charged at the applicable vehicle rate; there is no additional passenger charge simply because more people are travelling within the vehicle capacity.
