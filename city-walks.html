Simple Pricing

City Walk Pricing

Walking Tours Are Charged Per Person

  • Nairobi Highlights Walk: KES 2,500 per person
  • Nairobi History & Culture Walk: KES 2,500 per person
  • Nairobi Food & Local Life Walk: KES 3,500 per person

City walks are walking experiences, therefore vehicle and passenger vehicle charges do not apply.

If you require transport before or after your walk, you can separately request a vehicle transfer through Stema Trans.
