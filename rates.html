Know Before You Book

What's Included?

Generally Included

  • Private vehicle for the selected category
  • Professional driver
  • Vehicle operating time within the booked period
  • Normal Nairobi city service
  • Private service rather than shared transport

Generally Separate

  • National park entry / conservation fees
  • Museum and attraction entrance fees
  • Special activity charges
  • Overnight driver accommodation on multi-day trips
  • Extra hours beyond the booked period
  • Special long-distance itineraries
