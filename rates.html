Vehicle-based tour and transfer rates are charged per vehicle, not per passenger.

Entrance fees for Nairobi National Park, museums, conservancies and other attractions are separate unless your quotation states otherwise.

Custom Trips

Longer journeys, multi-day trips and itineraries outside Nairobi are quoted according to distance, duration, vehicle and itinerary.

Booking
