Important Information

Per Vehicle

All vehicle-based tour and transfer prices are charged per vehicle, regardless of whether the vehicle carries one passenger or its normal passenger capacity.

Attraction Fees

Entrance fees to museums, parks, conservancies and other attractions are normally paid separately unless your booking specifically states that they are included.

Longer Trips

Trips outside Nairobi, multi-day tours and safari itineraries are quoted separately according to distance, duration, vehicle type and itinerary.

Custom Bookings

If your itinerary does not fit the standard rates, contact us for a customized quotation based on your exact requirements.
