Experience Nairobi Beyond the Ordinary

Nairobi is a city of contrasts — modern buildings, historic streets, green spaces, vibrant markets, local food, culture and everyday Kenyan life.

STEMA TRANS helps visitors experience the city through guided tours, walking experiences and reliable private transportation.
