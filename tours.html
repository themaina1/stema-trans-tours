How Tour Pricing Works

Vehicle-Based Tours Are Charged Per Vehicle

Stema Trans vehicle-based tours are charged per vehicle, within the vehicle's normal passenger capacity.

Vehicle categories include: Sedan, SUV, Tour Van and Tour Land Cruiser.

Attraction entrance fees, park fees and other activity charges are separate unless a particular package specifically states that they are included.

For example, if four people travel together in one SUV, the transport charge is for the SUV, not four separate vehicle charges.
