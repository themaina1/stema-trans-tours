How Tour Pricing Works

Private Vehicle Tours

Stema Trans vehicle-based tours are charged per vehicle, not per passenger, within the applicable vehicle capacity.

Typical vehicle options include: Sedan, SUV, Tour Van and Tour Land Cruiser.

Attraction entrance fees, park fees and activity fees are charged separately unless the selected package specifically states that they are included.

This means you are not charged a separate passenger transport fee simply because additional guests are travelling within the vehicle's capacity.
